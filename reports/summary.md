**Health and Deprivation Across Ireland's Communities (Python, pandas, scikit-learn):** automated pipeline pulling five Census 2022 tables for all 3,420 Electoral Divisions from the CSO API, with automatic quality checks; population-weighted regression with robust confidence intervals, random forest validated on held-out counties (out-of-sample R² 0.01 for age alone vs 0.63 with social factors), k-means community profiles and ED-level maps; findings written for policymakers.
